The 4C's, Simplified

Know before
you buy.

Diamonds graded same as mined · IGI certified
C

Carat

Carat measures weight. Compare proportions and cut alongside carat to understand how a diamond will look in its setting.

C

Color

D (colorless) through H (near-colorless). All Celestyn stones D-H grade. Anything below H shows warmth.

C

Clarity

Clarity is assessed under 10x magnification. We can help you choose a stone with the appearance and clarity grade you prefer.

C

Cut

The only C controlled by human craft. Ideal and Excellent cuts only. This is where brilliance lives.

Our Story

Two cities.
One standard.

Celestyn Jewels is a partnership built on a simple thesis: the diamond supply chain has too many hands, and every hand takes a margin. We removed them.

Our Surat partner sources directly from the world's largest CVD lab-grown diamond growers. No brokers. No distributors. No legacy markup.

From Sydney, we handle the atelier experience — sales, custom design, IGI verification, aftercare. Every diamond arrives certified, fully insured, backed by our lifetime warranty.
